# Storylines

**A. The proof is cheaper than the fake (picked).** Device: life before and after, then a number lands.
Form: real product footage with punch-ins, broken by two bright number worlds. A real curl commit ends
the bug bounty, real slop titles flood in, then ProofGate disproves one claim by claim on the live site,
SlopBench lands "0 of 148", and the FixBench agent turns a real CVE's test from red to green. Picked
because it carries both halves of the product with real screens and real numbers.

**B. One CVE's journey.** Device: one object across apps. Form: one continuous take. A single 2026 curl
CVE travels from its advisory through triage into the fix agent, and the camera never cuts. Strong
spine, but it drops the slop story that motivates the product.

**C. Numbers as the argument.** Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat. Giant kinetic
numbers (49, 0/148, 11/27, $0.007) with the product appearing only in flashes. Bold, but it shows the
product working too little for a hackathon demo.
